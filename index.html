How we bake

The slow way

Three steps, three days. We don’t add speed. We add time, the cheapest ingredient we have and the only one you can taste.

  1. Day 1 · 3 pm

    Mix

    Flour, water, salt and a scoop of Gloria, our starter. Nothing else. Small batches, mixed by hand.

  2. Day 1 to 3

    Wait 48 hours

    Two days in the cold room. Wild yeast makes it rise; lactic bacteria and koji enzymes make it taste like something.

  3. Day 3 · 4:30 am

    Bake

    Into the stone-deck oven with a burst of steam. Out comes a crust that keeps crackling for twenty minutes.
